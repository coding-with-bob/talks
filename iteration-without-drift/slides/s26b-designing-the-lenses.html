Designing the lenses

the roster

Lenses, not redundancy

One perspective per agent. Three agents reading the same way is one review paid for three times.

An external model family is a lens too, and the only one that breaks self-reference.
mutation-style

Validate the lens itself

Seed a known defect into the corpus, then run the lens. Does it catch it?

An unvalidated lens produces confident silence.
Monitor lens yield too: sometimes you retire a lens, because the structure or the guidance improved.
the obligation

Evidence obligations

The reviewer must attach evidence to every claim, not just assert it.

LLM reviewers are non-deterministic: sometimes the claim is simply wrong. Evidence makes it re-checkable.

A lens is an artifact: design it, validate it, version it.
