Takeaways

Five things to take home

  1. 1

    Structure creates informationThe corpus is a system of relationships;
    the breakdown itself creates new, leverageable information.

  2. 2

    It serves both mindsHumans and LLMs get the same thing:
    understanding and navigation of the design space.

  3. 3

    The design can breathe, and can be verifiedStructure makes change safe and the result checkable.

  4. 4

    Route human attention by decision typeThe agent runs free on decided and derived;
    the human handles the genuinely new.

  5. 5

    Design for the LLM’s characteristicsEspecially the feedback loops:
    without triage and a stop rule, a neverending nitpicking loop.
